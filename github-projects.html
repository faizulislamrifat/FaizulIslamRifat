Turn Long Videos & Podcasts into Viral Captioned Shorts, Reels & TikToks

Free and open-source automated video clipping engine. Paste a YouTube, Twitch or Kick link (or upload a local file) and MAKLU CUT automatically scouts conversational peaks, formats clips into 9:16 vertical video, adds animated word-by-word captions with custom Bengali fonts, and auto-posts them to social channels.

  • AI-Powered Clip Scout analyzing conversational and emotional peaks
  • Bengali typography support: Hind Siliguri, Kalpurush, Noto Sans Bengali

  • Bouncy word-by-word animated captions with auto social posting
