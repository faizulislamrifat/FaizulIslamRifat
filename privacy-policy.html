Privacy Policy

Your privacy is paramount. This policy outlines how Faizul Islam Rifat collects, uses, and safeguards your data across our website and growth consultation services.

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to the official portfolio and consulting platform of Faizul Islam Rifat ("we," "our," or "us"). We provide high-retention video editing production, performance advertising management, and conversion web solutions for creators, startups, and modern businesses.

We are firmly committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have questions or concerns about this policy or our practices regarding your personal information, please contact us directly at info.faizulislamrifat@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to communicate with you, coordinate video strategy consultations, or deliver contracted client services.

  • Voluntarily Provided Information: When you book a strategy call or send an inquiry, we collect your name, email address, WhatsApp/phone number, preferred meeting platform, and project brief.
  • Technical & Browser Data: Like most modern websites, we may collect non-personally identifiable log data such as IP address, browser type, referring pages, and device specifications to maintain optimal site performance and security.
  • Cookies & Pixel Tags: We use standard privacy-respecting cookies and pixel tags (such as Meta Pixel and Google Analytics) solely for tracking audience reach, campaign attribution, and conversion efficiency.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use personal information collected via our website for legitimate business purposes described below:

  • To schedule, confirm, and host 1-on-1 video consultations via Google Meet or Zoom.
  • To deliver automated calendar invites, reminder emails, and instant WhatsApp/Telegram alerts regarding your scheduled slot.
  • To respond directly to project inquiries, provide customized proposals, and provide contracted video/marketing deliverables.
  • To protect our services from malicious spam, fraudulent submissions, and technical vulnerabilities.

⭐ We NEVER sell, lease, or monetize your personal information to third parties or data brokers under any circumstances.

4. Third-Party Integrations & Processors

To automate our scheduling system and communication securely, we interface with trusted industry-standard infrastructure:

  • Google Apps Script & Calendar API: To auto-generate Google Meet consultation slots.
  • Telegram Bot API: To send encrypted server-side push notification alerts for new incoming consultation requests.
  • WhatsApp Business: For direct, encrypted client communications when initiated by you.

Each of these providers maintains their own strict compliance standards (SOC 2, GDPR, ISO 27001).

5. Data Retention & Security

We apply modern organizational and technical security measures—including SSL/TLS 256-bit encryption and strict access controls—to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

We only retain your personal data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights under applicable data protection laws, including the right to request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal contact records.

To exercise any of these rights, simply email us at info.faizulislamrifat@gmail.com. We will process your request within 48 business hours.
